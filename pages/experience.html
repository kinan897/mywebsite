Co-op Placement

Mobile App Developer

CEC Career Education Council

Swift Xcode iOS Development UI/UX Collaboration

Overview

As an aspiring app developer with a keen interest in engineering and computer science, I gained hands-on experience at CEC Career Education Council. Through this co-op placement, I learned to develop mobile applications using Swift and Xcode — two of the most powerful tools in the app development industry. The experience deepened my understanding of the full app development lifecycle and gave me skills I plan to carry into my future career.

What I Did

  • Attended daily meetings at 9:30 AM and 12:30 PM to discuss project progress and development strategy alongside the team.
  • Learned to build mobile applications using Swift in Xcode, from first principles through practical, working features.
  • Gained hands-on experience implementing app features, debugging issues, and refining functionality based on testing and feedback.
  • Collaborated with team members to design and improve app interfaces, keeping the end-to-end user experience smooth and consistent.

What I Took Away

This placement built both technical skill and a strong work ethic — the two things that matter most in the fast-paced world of app development. It gave me a real, ground-level view of how an app actually gets built, not just how to write code in isolation. I'm looking to keep growing in this field, with the goal of making a lasting impact in tech.
