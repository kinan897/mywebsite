Bikini Bottom Beatdown

A SpongeBob-inspired 2D fighting game, built solo in Construct 3.

Construct 3 Event Sheets 2D Fighting Game

Overview

Bikini Bottom Beatdown is a SpongeBob-inspired fighting game where you play as SpongeBob, taking on a roster of corrupted Bikini Bottom characters — including Patrick and Squidward — one brawl at a time. Every fight comes down to managing a health bar: run it out and the battle restarts from the top.

How It Works

  • Normal attack: A fast, low-commitment hit for keeping pressure on an opponent.
  • Charge attack: Hold to wind up a stronger hit that trades speed for damage.
  • Jump: Used to dodge incoming attacks and reposition mid-fight.
  • Health bar: Every hit you take or land moves the fight — lose all your health and you restart the battle.
  • Final boss — Mr. Krabs: The strongest fight in the game. His arena has a hole in the middle of the map that drops you for a big chunk of health if you fall in, on top of everything he throws at you.

Built With

Built in Construct 3, with a full event-sheet-driven combat system handling attack states, hit detection, health, and a dedicated boss arena for the final fight against Mr. Krabs.
