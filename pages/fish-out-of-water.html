Fish Out of Water

A 2D action game · full Godot project + Windows build

Godot Engine GDScript 2D Windows

Overview

Fish Out of Water is a 2D platformer set in waters poisoned by radiation leaking from nearby factories, threatening every creature living in them. You play as a fish fighting back — breaching into the factories themselves to destroy them and stop the environmental damage at its source.

How It Works

  • Core moves: Jump and attack from the start, with new abilities unlocked as you progress.
  • Dash: Cross wide gaps that a normal jump can't clear.
  • Wall jump: Chain off walls to reach higher areas.
  • Enemies: A mix of ground and flying enemies guarding each factory, each requiring a different approach.

Built With

Built in the Godot Engine using GDScript, with a full custom set of scenes for the player, enemies, breakable environment pieces, and an in-game dialogue/NPC system.
